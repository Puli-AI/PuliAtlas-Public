# 01 — Product Principles

## Connections before categories

Folders and menus help people enter a body of knowledge. Explicit relationships help them understand it. PuliWeave supports both.

## One card, one page

A card should make one coherent idea readable without becoming a miniature report. It may link to supporting cards, publications and evidence.

## The graph is a view, not a database

Relationships live in structured source data. The graph visualises them; it does not invent or own them.

## Preview, then hold

Hover or focus previews a card and its neighbourhood. Click, press or keyboard selection holds that state and produces a shareable URL.

## Static first

The first publication target is a versioned static build. This keeps hosting simple, fast and portable. Optional services such as comments, search APIs or AI assistants sit behind clear boundaries.

## Public by deliberate export

A user’s private graph must never become public merely because the renderer can read it. Publication uses an explicit inclusion policy and produces a separate public artifact.

## Accessible relationships

Every graph action needs a keyboard, touch and text equivalent. Reduced-motion users receive stable transitions rather than constant movement.

## Credit sources and influences

PuliWeave acknowledges the products and thinkers that influenced its design while using its own implementation, brand and information architecture.
