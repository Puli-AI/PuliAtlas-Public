# 05 — Credits and Acknowledgements

PuliAtlas is an original Puli Consulting concept and implementation informed by several important products and intellectual traditions.

## 37signals

[37signals](https://37signals.com/) demonstrates disciplined typography, persistent conventional navigation and memorable numbered editorial structures. PuliAtlas takes inspiration from that clarity, not from copying its source code, content or brand.

## Roam Research

[Roam Research](https://roamresearch.com/) helped popularise graph-centred networked thought, bidirectional page links and block references. These ideas influenced the connected-note presentation and importer goals.

PuliAtlas is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Roam Research. Compatibility should be claimed only for tested public export formats.

## Sönke Ahrens and *How to Take Smart Notes*

Sönke Ahrens’ [*How to Take Smart Notes*](https://www.soenkeahrens.de/en/takesmartnotes) helped articulate the discipline of focused notes whose value grows through meaningful connections.

## Zettelkasten and Niklas Luhmann

PuliAtlas acknowledges the broader Zettelkasten tradition and Niklas Luhmann’s work with a linked slip-box. The [Niklas Luhmann Archive at Bielefeld University](https://www.uni-bielefeld.de/fakultaeten/soziologie/forschung/luhmann-archiv/) is an important scholarly resource.

## Independence

Acknowledgement does not imply endorsement or partnership. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. PuliAtlas uses its own brand, code, content model and interaction decisions.
